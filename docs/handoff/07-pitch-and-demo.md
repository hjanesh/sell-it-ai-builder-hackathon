# 07: Pitch and demo (WIP)

## Mapping to the judging criteria
| Criterion | Our answer |
|---|---|
| **Problem & Insight** | Buyers trust the listing where the seller made an effort. Most sellers don't, because it's tedious. Same product, 20 listings: the clean, detailed one wins. |
| **Ambition & Originality** | Not another marketplace: a selling layer across every channel Indians actually use, including WhatsApp groups and home businesses that no platform's AI reaches. |
| **Agentic Depth** | It's a selling agent: it looks at the photos, decides what evidence is missing, asks only the questions that matter, prices, produces assets for each channel and publishes. Next: answers buyers and negotiates within the seller's lowest price. |
| **Traction** | `/stats` live: real users, real listings, real feedback quotes. |
| **Demo & Polish** | Mobile-first, fast, a before/after photo moment. |

## Stage demo (draft, ~90s)
1. Show a real, bad OLX-style listing (blurry photo, one line).
2. Snap a judge's phone or laptop live, and add a 5-second voice note in Hinglish.
3. Show the AI working: detects the item, asks two smart questions, studio photos appear (before → after).
4. Swipe through the OLX, Instagram and WhatsApp packs, then share to WhatsApp live.
5. QR code to the listing page on the judge's phone.
6. Close on `/stats`: "N people listed ₹X worth of stuff today."

## VC narrative
- **Wedge:** free listing assistant for ordinary sellers (viral through WhatsApp shares).
- **Revenue:** Instagram shops, resellers and home businesses (bulk mode, brand kits, catalogue posters); later,
  partnerships with platforms for direct posting and lead-gen for recommerce companies.
- **Why now:** vision models read model numbers and condition; Indian-language speech-to-text is good; segmentation is cheap.
- **Risk we name ourselves:** platforms building this natively (Meta already has in the US). Answer: multi-channel +
  India context + WhatsApp commerce.
- **Pine Labs angle:** "The OS for commerce is agents". We're the agent on the seller's side; payments (UPI reserve) are a natural next step.
