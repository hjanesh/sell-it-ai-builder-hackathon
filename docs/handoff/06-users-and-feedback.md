# 06: 20 users + feedback (WIP)

**Requirement:** 20 real users, public is fine, who **actively use** the product and **give feedback**.
Traction weighs ~25% (Round 1) and ~30% (Round 2).

## Build it into the product
- **Light identity** before first export: name + WhatsApp number or email. No passwords.
- **Feedback sheet** after every export or share: 👍/👎 · "Where will you post?" (OLX / Marketplace / Instagram / WhatsApp / other) · optional comment.
- Ask again on the listing page: "Posted it? Got enquiries?"
- **Count active users only:** people who finished a listing. Judges may check.
- **`/stats` page:** users, listings, ₹ value listed, listings by type, 👍 rate, latest feedback quotes. Show it at the table.

## Where users come from
1. **Home bakers and home businesses in society WhatsApp groups.** They post often, the WhatsApp poster is
   immediately useful, and it's the fastest route.
2. **Friends and family groups:** "Selling anything? Make a listing that actually sells, in 60 seconds."
3. **People moving out in Bengaluru:** flat and move-out groups sell furniture and appliances constantly.
4. **In the hall:** builders have gadgets, bags and other things to try it on.
5. **Instagram thrift stores:** DM a few with a free bulk trial (they're also the future paying customer).
