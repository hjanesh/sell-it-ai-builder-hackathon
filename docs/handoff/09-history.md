# 09: How we got here (for context)

1. **DemoDay (dropped).** First idea: paste your product URL and get a narrated, brand-styled demo video
   (Playwright capture → Remotion motion blocks → ElevenLabs voice clone), launched to the other teams when they
   need submission videos. Track would have been AI for Builders.
   Dropped for ThriftIt: simpler to build, works on a phone, and users aren't limited to people at the event.
   (We'd found a timing problem too: teams need the video before ~17:00, so demand would peak early.)
2. **ThriftIt v1 (appraiser).** Snap your wardrobe or attic → AI values each item in ₹ → listing → share to WhatsApp
   Status. Ideas from that phase that may still be useful: a "Wrapped"-style share card ("My room is worth ₹18,400"),
   choosing the best place to sell each item (Cashify / Instagram / OLX / kabadiwala / donate), a negotiation agent.
3. **ThriftIt v2 (listing assistant).** Hemanth's reframe: the real value is **listing quality = buyer trust**.
   Gather details (as few or as many as the seller has), clean up the photos, write copy for each platform,
   download or post anywhere. Platform-agnostic.
4. **ThriftIt v3 (sell anything, current).** The team widened it to *everyone who sells*: pre-owned, new, and
   homemade / small-batch (home bakers etc. in WhatsApp society groups), across OLX, Marketplace, Instagram, WhatsApp.
   Voice input via ElevenLabs. Hosting on Render or Replit.
