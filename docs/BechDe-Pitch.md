## 1. The problem

Millions of Indian homes have something to sell.

Sometimes it's **old stuff** — the laptop replaced two phones ago, the pram the baby outgrew in four months, silk sarees that haven't left the cupboard in a decade.

Sometimes it's **something made at home** — dosa batter, brownies, crochet baskets, paintings, hand-poured candles, pickles, tiffin service.

**They almost never actually sell.** The stuff sits in cupboards or gets given away. The dosa batter mum posts once a month, badly, in the society WhatsApp group, and never grows.

Why?

Because selling anything from your home demands five hard decisions, in a row, with no help:
- *What exactly am I selling?* (positioning)
- *What's it worth?* (pricing)
- *How do I show it off?* (photos, copy)
- *Where do I even post it?* (platform choice)
- *What language — English, Hindi, Marathi?*

Each decision alone is small. Together they're paralysing. So nothing gets listed. **The hesitation gap is the problem.**

## 3. BechDe — the starter kit

**One product. One persona. One flow.**

**Persona:** anyone with something to sell from home — whether that's an old guitar or a batch of fresh ladoos. We don't distinguish *reseller* vs *maker*. We ask one question: *"What are you selling?"*

**Promise:** your first listing live, in your language, with a fair price and good photos, in 60 seconds — on the platform that's actually right for *that* item.

**Explicitly not:** a full D2C storefront or a 24/7 shop. BechDe respects that most home sellers don't want constant orders. Every listing can be marked *Weekly batches*, *By request*, or *One-off* — so a mother selling dosa batter on Tuesdays isn't buried under daily DMs.

## 4. The moat — AI for all 4 Ps

Every marketer learns the 4 Ps: **Product, Price, Promotion, Place.** Big brands have teams for each. Home sellers have none.

BechDe is an AI marketing cofounder for the home seller's first listing. One photo in, all 4 Ps out:

| | What AI does | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| **Product** | Identifies the item, extracts specs, asks follow-ups ("send me the About screen"), writes a sharp 1-line positioning | Removes the "what am I even selling" paralysis |
| **Price** | Pulls 5 real comparable listings from live sources (OLX, Marketplace, Cashify, Amazon used, nearby society groups), suggests a fair-range price with citations | Kills the "I'll be lowballed" fear with hard data |
| **Promotion** | Generates 3 studio-quality photos + ad copy in 2–4 Indian languages, formatted for each channel | Removes the "I need design skills" barrier |
| **Place** | Recommends the right channel(s) for *this* item — e.g. *crochet → Instagram + Etsy; dosa batter → society WhatsApp + local FB group; old laptop → OLX + Cashify* | Removes the "where should I post" paralysis |

**No single tool today does all four.** OLX only gives Place. Canva only touches Promotion. ChatGPT writes copy but can't ground price in real listings. BechDe is the first wrapper around all four — with real citations and real multilingual output.

That's the moat: **the complete first-listing kit, grounded in real data, in the user's language, aware of their platform of choice.**

## 5. Product flow

1. **Snap** one photo of what you're selling.
2. **AI identifies** it. If it needs more info — *"Open your laptop → About → send me that screen"* or *"How many litres is this batter?"* — it asks.
3. **4 Ps unlock on screen:**
   - *Product card* — what we think you're selling + positioning line
   - *Price card* — fair range with 5 live citations you can click
   - *Promotion preview* — one sample photo + one caption
   - *Place suggestion* — top 2 channels for this item
4. **🔒 ₹49 unlocks the full Starter Kit:**
   - 3 studio-restaged photos
   - Ad copy in 2 languages × 4 channel formats (WhatsApp, Instagram, FB Marketplace, OLX)
   - Availability setting: *Weekly batches / By request / One-off*
   - A public listing page you can forward
5. **One-tap share** to WhatsApp + Instagram story + copy-to-clipboard for OLX/Marketplace.

Every step has an obvious next click. No dead ends.

## 7. Honest critique — what could kill this

> *Pressure-test, don't nod along.*

### Real risks

1. **Grounded pricing is the moat — and it's fragile.** Serper/Brave over OLX/Marketplace returns irrelevant snippets ~30% of the time. One dead citation on stage kills credibility.
   **Fix:** 5 citations, filter by `₹` presence + successful URL fetch, prefer Cashify/Amazon-renewed for electronics (structured pages). For handmade/food, maintain a small seeded database of 50 local comparables overnight.

2. **"Studio photos" of a real item are harder than they sound.** Nano Banana can swap backgrounds for simple objects. For a dosa batter container or a crochet basket? Expect surprises.
   **Fix:** background replace + relight only — don't regenerate the product. If output is iffy by 15:00, fall back to "AI-enhanced original" (upscale + white background).

3. **₹49 one-time has low paying willingness** — OLX is free. The real paying user is the home maker who returns weekly.
   **Fix:** Mode A (resale) free + Mode B (makers) ₹49 per listing OR ₹99/month unlimited. Different hooks, same product. Decide tonight.

4. **"Posting" is a half-promise.** We can't get Marketplace/OLX API write access in 12 hours.
   **Fix:** be honest in copy — *"We write the listing. You post with one tap."* Our actual distribution channel is the public listing page + WhatsApp.

5. **The 4Ps framing is academic — judges might call it over-engineered.**
   **Fix:** Don't use the term "4 Ps" in the pitch. Say *"what to call it, what to charge, what to show, where to post."* The 4Ps are the internal spine, not the external vocabulary.

6. **Thick competition.** OLX has AI listing writing. Meta is rolling out Marketplace AI enhancement. Dukaan + QuickSell serve micro-sellers.
   **Our wedge:** multilingual + WhatsApp-native + the hesitation-breaking UX + the one place that ties all 4 Ps together with grounded data. None of the incumbents nail this combination.

7. **The "not always open" nuance is a feature nobody else has — but it's invisible unless we show it.**
   **Fix:** demo the *Weekly batches / By request / One-off* toggle on the dosa batter example. It's the detail that lands with every WhatsApp aunty.

### Scope cuts worth considering

| Cut | Save | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| Drop image regeneration, keep only background-swap + upscale | 2–3 hours + removes AI-slop risk | Weaker demo visuals |
| Ship 2 languages (En + Hi); add Marathi/Tamil only after 16:00 | 1 hour | Narrower regional reach |
| Drop OLX/Marketplace deep links; only WhatsApp + public listing page | 2 hours | Less "posting" story — but more honest |
| Drop phone OTP, use a nickname + photo-only session | 1 hour | Lose return-user tracking |
| ₹49 one-time now; add subscription later | 0.5 hour | Weaker LTV story — but faster to ship |

### Recommended cuts for 12h

- **Only WhatsApp + public listing page** for distribution. Marketplace/OLX = "coming next week."
- **English + Hindi tomorrow.** Marathi/Tamil as a stretch after 16:00.
- **Background swap only, no product regeneration.** Non-negotiable.
- **₹49 per listing + a visible "Monthly ₹99 for makers" button** that just shows a Razorpay link — enough to prove intent for the pitch.
